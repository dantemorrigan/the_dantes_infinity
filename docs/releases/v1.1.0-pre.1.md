# The Dante’s Infinity 1.1.0-pre.1 (pre-release)

**The Fury of Doom** and everything since 1.0.7. A pre-release for testing: Minecraft 26.1.2 (Fabric Loader ≥ 0.20.1, Fabric API 0.155.3+26.1.2, Java 25). Put `infinity_gauntlet-1.1.0-pre.1.jar` into `mods/` on the server and for every player; all must use the same jar (new network packets). Back up your world first. Existing worlds are migrated in place (the castle gets its lift, old battlefield fires are put out).

## 🏰 Latveria and Doctor Doom
- A new dimension: a walled city of ~250 furnished houses, a giant statue of Doom, Castle Doom with rooms in every tower, the resistance questline (Old Mechanic Radovan, Tavern Keeper Mirko, the Citizen Cloak, the Generator Disruptor), a stealth hunt (searchlights, Interceptors, witnesses, house sieges where Doombots climb to your floor and punch through the walls) and the throne-room fight against Doom in three stages. The sky, the music and the screen grade are Latveria's own.
- **The way in** is the Space Stone portal wheel: pick Latveria and a green-rimmed portal opens. Space Stone flight works there.
- **The generator** sits in a vault under the castle: take the new anti-gravity lift in the east wing. The generator is marked with a label, sparks and a hint. Switching it off powers down every Doombot outside the throne hall.
- Doom, the Doombots, the searchlights and the hunt treat creative players as targets too. Doom pulls down anyone who keeps out of his reach.
- **The Gauntlet fight.** If Doom takes an incomplete gauntlet from you, he carries it to a battlefield in the Overworld, kills Thanos and faces you wearing it, using the powers of its stones: meteors from the sun or moon, time, reality, mind and soul, paced and telegraphed. It has its own soundtrack, boss bar, longer cutscenes and a compass that comes back if you die or leave.
- Doom's stones can never be duplicated by the theft or by his death (new smoke test `DoomStoneDupTest`).

## 💀 The Temple of Soul
- The **Red Skull**, keeper of the Soul Stone, levitates on the summit and talks to whoever right-clicks him. While the stone waits, the land round the mountain turns to **Vormir** (greyer, dimmer, ash-orange, falling ash); it lifts and he departs once the stone is taken.

## 🖱 Controls and feel
- **Mouse-wheel ability switch:** hold Left Alt and scroll (config `abilityScrollMode`: `modifier`, `always`, `off`); the choice changes at once, a strip of neighbouring abilities glides under the crosshair. Both ability wheels (the gauntlet's and Doom's) scroll too, with frame-rate independent easing and an instant stone switch on hover.
- A worn gauntlet now heals its bearer faster the more stones it holds (0.6 to 4.5 HP a second).

## ⚡ Performance and fixes
- Latveria: allocation-free sky, lighter searchlight beams, townsfolk that think less when nobody is near, slow sections of the server tick logged (`Latveria tick section ... took N ms`). Several crashes and cutscene glitches fixed (the energy dome flicker and lag, the hand in cutscenes, Doom and Thanos sunk into the floor, the gauntlet already on Doom's arm in the theft scene).

## 🌐 Texts
- Texts and both READMEs are brought up to date; `docs/VOICE_LINES.md` lists every Doom and Doombot line for the voice-over.

This is a pre-release: please report anything odd, especially lag in Latveria and the final fight.
